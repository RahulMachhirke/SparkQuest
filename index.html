📋 Round 1: Rapid Fire — Competition Guidelines

  • Format: 25–30 technical questions on programming fundamentals, core technical concepts, and general computing knowledge.
  • Question Types: Includes both single-correct multiple choice (🔘) and multiple-select questions (☑️ choose all that apply).
  • Speed Scoring: Correct answers submitted faster receive higher points (up to 1,000 points per question) plus streak multiplier bonuses.
  • Tie-Breaking Rule: If two teams tie on total points, the team with the lower overall completion time ranks higher.
